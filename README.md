## Approach

For each area I aim to document:

1. **Objective** — what I was trying to understand or achieve.
2. **Methodology** — how I approached the problem.
3. **Tools** — what I used and why.
4. **Observations** — relevant output and what it indicated.
5. **Lessons learned** — what I would repeat or change next time.

Sensitive information, credentials and direct solutions to active training challenges will not be published.
